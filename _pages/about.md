I am currently a third-year Master’s student at Northwestern Polytechnical University, with a research focus on myocardial aging. My major is bioinformatics.

After obtaining my bachelor’s degree, I worked in biotech companies, including Selleckchem, for six years as a sales representative. 

Since 2019, I have been working as a research assistant in the School of Basic Medical Sciences.

My research interests focus on the clinical application of multi-omics approaches, including transcriptomics, proteomics, and metabolomics.

To date, I have published three papers as a corresponding author in CAS Q1 journals.
